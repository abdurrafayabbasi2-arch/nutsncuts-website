Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering pure dry fruits, spices, custom pack weights, and delivery across Pakistan.

What items can I buy from Nuts N Cuts by Pakeeza?
Nuts N Cuts offers 56+ premium pantry staples including handpicked dry fruits (Salted Iranian Pistachios, American & Irani Almonds, Walnuts, Cashews, Chilgoza, Dried Figs/Injeer), 100% pure whole and ground kitchen spices (Haldi, Zeera, Lal Mirch, Garam Masala), imported Saudi dates (Ajwa, Medjool, Mabroom), and organic wellness seeds (Chia seeds, Kalonji, Flax seeds).
How does custom weight and Rupee ordering work?
You are not limited to fixed 1kg packs! You can choose standard 250g, 500g, 1kg sizes, or use our Rupee Budget Calculator to order any exact amount (e.g. Rs. 300 of almonds or Rs. 500 of mixed dry fruit). We calculate the exact grams, freshly weigh, and heat-seal your order cleanly.
What are the delivery charges and delivery times across Pakistan?
For the Twin Cities (Islamabad & Rawalpindi), rider delivery is a flat Rs. 150 (delivered within 24-48 hours). For all other cities in Pakistan, courier delivery is Rs. 250. Orders above Rs. 4,000 receive 100% FREE nationwide home delivery.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan. Customers can also pay in advance via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay, or direct Online Bank Transfer to account 03333415977.
How do I place an order?
Browse the catalog, add products in your desired pack sizes or Rupee amounts to your cart, fill your delivery address in checkout, and click "Confirm on WhatsApp". Your formatted order invoice will be sent directly to our official WhatsApp desk (+92 333 3415977) for instant dispatch confirmation.
